# DAFTARI

### Your business. Your numbers. Your insight.

DAFTARI is an AI-powered business management app designed for small businesses.

It helps business owners record daily operations, understand their numbers, manage inventory, and turn business data into practical insights.

## 🚀 Features

- 📊 Business Dashboard
- 💰 Sales & Purchases
- 📦 Inventory Management
- 💸 Expense Tracking
- 🗑️ Waste / Damaged Items
- 👥 Customer Management
- 📈 Reports & Date-Based Analysis
- 🤖 AI Business Analyst
- 📱 Offline data management

## 🤖 AI Business Analyst

DAFTARI uses Google Gemini AI to analyze the business data selected by the owner.

The AI can help identify:

- Best-selling products
- Slow-moving inventory
- Changes in revenue and profit
- Unusual expenses
- Product profitability
- Inventory patterns
- Business trends
- Potential areas that need attention

The selected date range is respected when generating an analysis.

## 📱 Offline First

Core business operations work without an internet connection.

Business data is stored locally on the device.

Internet access is only required for AI-powered analysis.
