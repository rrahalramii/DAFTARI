# Daftari competition demo script

## 1. Open the app
Show the Daftari logo and dashboard.

## 2. Show offline data entry
Open Inventory and add a product. Record a sale and an expense. Explain that these records are stored locally in Room and do not require an internet connection.

## 3. Show the important part
Open AI Analyst.

Select a period covering the seeded demo data.

Say:
> “I am not asking AI to simply summarize my sales. Daftari gives it the entire business picture for this period.”

Tap Analyze.

## 4. Highlight cross-data reasoning
Look for findings about:
- LED headlights: purchases vs sales vs remaining stock.
- Electricity: expense behavior relative to business activity.
- Waste/damage.
- Margin and profit.

The model is instructed to show the numbers behind findings and to label uncertain causes as things to check.

## 5. Follow-up
Ask:
> “Which products have high inventory but weak sales, and what should I investigate before buying more?”

Then:
> “Why did my profit change?”

This demonstrates that Daftari is an interactive analyst rather than a static accounting dashboard.
